## Why not fix it at home?

Home drying efforts often leave damp spots and rot. Wet wool mildews without full drying, and natural dyes smear with improper care, so effective water damage restoration is tough without know-how.

## How we restore water-damaged rugs

Water damage restoration salvages hand-knotted or woven rugs, typically wool or silk, by removing moisture and treating the affected areas, to dry and save the rug.
