## Why not fix it at home?

Clumsy home fixes with glue or scissors make fringe damage worse. Loose knots unravel further with improper handling, and mismatched materials stand out, so fringe repair is hard to get right without the skill.

## How we repair fringe

Fringe repair restores the knotted or woven edges of hand-knotted rugs, typically wool or silk, by resecuring or replacing worn threads. It strengthens and renews the rug's edges.
