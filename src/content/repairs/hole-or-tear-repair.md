## Why not fix it at home?

Hasty home fixes with glue or odd threads leave a rug lumpy and frail. Shaky stitches unravel fast, and uneven patches warp the rug, so a solid repair is hard to get right without the skill.

## How we repair holes and tears

Hole or tear repair patches gaps or splits in hand-knotted or woven rugs by weaving in matching threads to close the damage. Done properly, it mends the rug and fortifies it so the repair lasts.
