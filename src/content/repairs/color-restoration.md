## Why not fix it at home?

Harsh cleaning attempts strip natural dyes and leave them dull and uneven. Strong soaps bleach delicate dyes further, and uneven rinsing leaves patchy spots, so proper color restoration is tough without expertise.

## How we restore color

Color restoration revives the vibrancy of hand-knotted or woven rugs, often made of wool or silk, by carefully treating faded or washed-out hues.
