## Why not fix it at home?

Hasty home repairs with tape or stitching leave rugs frayed and unstable. Uneven knots or weak materials pull apart quickly, so proper edge binding is hard without the right tools.

## How we bind edges

Edge binding reinforces the borders of hand-knotted or woven rugs, often wool or silk, by wrapping threads to secure loose ends. It protects and steadies the rug's borders.
