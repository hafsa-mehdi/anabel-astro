## Why not fix it at home?

Shaky home repairs with mismatched threads only worsen the damage, leaving rugs weak and unsightly. Torn wool frays further with clumsy stitching, and without the right tools the structure suffers.

## How we reweave rugs

Rug reweaving mends tears, holes or worn patches in hand-knotted or woven rugs by recreating the original weave with matching wool or silk threads. It is done by hand, to restore the rug and stop further unraveling.
